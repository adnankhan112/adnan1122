My Assignment: Video, Audio and Image in HTML

This page demonstrates how to embed video, audio, and images using HTML.

1. Image Tag (<img>)

To use your own image, set src="your-image.jpg".

2. Video Tag (<video>)

Replace "my-video.mp4" with your actual video file's name/path. It won't play here since no file is uploaded yet — once you add your real video alongside this file, it will work.

3. Audio Tag (<audio>)

Similarly, replace "my-audio.mp3" with your own audio file's name.

What the Code Looks Like:

<img src="pic.jpg" alt="My Image">

<video controls>
  <source src="video.mp4" type="video/mp4">
</video>

<audio controls>
  <source src="song.mp3" type="audio/mpeg">
</audio>
